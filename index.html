SMALL SCOPE. REAL WORKFLOW.

Evidence, then a decision

01

Start with structured evidence

Import bank transactions and a receipt index. The demo includes 12 synthetic transactions and 10 synthetic receipt records. PDF contents are not read.

02

Apply inspectable rules

Exact signed USD amount, dates within the chosen tolerance, and normalized merchant overlap of at least 50%. Only a unique, exact merchant match with no shared receipt is marked matched. This is a heuristic, not AI confidence.

03

Keep judgment with the reviewer

Confirm a candidate, record an exclusion with a reason, or mark evidence requested. A receipt cannot be allocated twice. A matching record does not prove business purpose, tax treatment, or document authenticity.

04

Leave with a useful action

Download a review register and an editable client request. No accounting entries or emails are sent. This prototype has no LLM, external integrations, or server-side storage.
